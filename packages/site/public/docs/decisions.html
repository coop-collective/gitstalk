Decision cards and reconcile live

Most clashes between agents are small, and Gitstalk handles them. A person is asked only when two changes genuinely want different behaviour, with both sides laid out.

From a red to a person, in order

  1. Informed repair. A red pre-land check goes back to the bean's author with the failing tests and the landed beans it collided with: their titles, intents and what they changed. The author usually fixes it in one round.
  2. Reconcile. If the same test fails again, a test author looks at every landed task behind the failing tests. When a test only pins an old value that the other task legitimately changed (an order total before shipping was added), it updates the test and the bean lands.
  3. Decision card. When the two specs contradict each other, the bean stops and a card opens: both intents, both tests, and what each side would keep. The bean is parked with the reason (needs a person: two specs disagree) and costs nothing while it waits.
  4. After the decision. By default the landed winner is kept, the loser's tests are amended to the decided spec, and the loser is re-executed under it.

Pairs declared as coupled get their card before either starts, rather than after a red. A bean that keeps failing without progress is parked too, so one hopeless bean never holds a run open.

Why this order

  • Re-executing the loser under the decided spec shipped all 40 tasks in three clean runs, against 35 without it, at no extra cost.
  • Reverting the winner instead never worked (0 of 3): other beans had already built on it.
  • Reconciling before raising a card stopped cards for clashes that were only stale test values; the one reconcile in that race that ended in a contradiction was a real one.

Where it runs today

What Status
Informed repair, reconcile, cards and parking in the engine (every race on the hosted service) live
Cards on pushed beans: the push prints decision card D001: …. Pushed beans carry no separate acceptance tests yet, so reconcile has nothing to amend and a contradiction goes straight to a card or a park. live
Answering a repository's cards on the web, by the owner or a maintainer live
Card tools over MCP; routing a card to the spec's owner coming soon
