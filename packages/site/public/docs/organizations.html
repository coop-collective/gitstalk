Organizations live

An organization is a shared home for repositories, secrets and members. Its owners decide what members may do with every repository, so you do not invite people one repository at a time.

Create one

Choose New organization from the + New menu in the header. Give it a handle, a name and an optional description. You become its owner.

Roles and base permission

Role May
owner Everything, including other owners and deleting the organization. The last owner cannot leave.
admin Settings, secrets, members and every repository, as its owner. Cannot grant owner.
member The base permission on every repository. Creates repositories unless only admins may.
viewer The base permission, at most read.

The base permission is what members get on every repository without being invited to it: none (the default), read or write. With none, members and viewers reach only the repositories they are invited to, plus the organization's internal and public ones. Owners and admins reach every repository.

Who can do what

Who Private Internal Public
Owner, admin Owner Owner Owner
Member or viewer, base none (default) Not found Read Read
Member, base read or write Read or write Read or write Read or write
Viewer, base read or write Read Read Read
Outside the organization Not found Not found Read

Not found is a real 404: the repository looks as if it does not exist. A collaborator role on one repository adds to this, and the stronger one counts. The same rule decides access over HTTPS, SSH, MCP and the web.

Organization settings

Open an organization from the menu under your avatar, then Settings. Each section is its own page with its own Save. Owners and admins see all six; other members see Secrets and variables, and Danger zone to leave.

Section What it does Who sees it
General Name, description and the icon (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF up to 2 MB, cropped square; without one you get the handle's first letters). See the upload rules. owner, admin
Members and invitations Invite by handle with a role (member by default; only owners grant owner), change roles, remove people, cancel invitations. Invitees accept on their Home. owner, admin
Repository defaults The base permission, who may create repositories, and whether new ones start private or public. owner, admin
Secrets and variables Actions secrets and variables shared across the organization's repositories. See Secrets and variables. everyone in the organization; owners and admins manage
Audit log The last 100 events, newest first, times in UTC. owner, admin
Danger zone Leave the organization (everyone; the last owner must name another owner first). Owners can delete it once it owns no repositories, by typing its handle. everyone; delete for owners

What the audit log records

The log records: the organization created and its settings changed; members invited, invitations cancelled, accepted or declined; roles changed; members removed or who left; repositories created in it; and repositories moved in or out.

Repositories in an organization

  • Create one from New repository by choosing the organization as the owner. Where you may create depends on the organization's rule above.
  • Move one in or out from the repository's Settings, Danger zone (transfer). You can move your own repository to an organization where you are an owner or admin, or an organization's repository to yourself or another organization you administer. History, beans, collaborators and deploy tokens move with it, and the old address keeps working. See Renamed and moved.
  • An internal repository moved to a person becomes private, because only organizations have internal repositories. Moved to another organization it stays internal to that one.

Organization secrets

Each organization secret or variable reaches one of three sets of repositories: all, private only (which means not public, so internal ones too, as on GitHub), or selected repositories you pick. A repository's own entry of the same name wins over the organization's. Values are typed once and never shown again. Details, including the pre-land switch, are on Secrets and variables.
